# OpenDementia — Screen for targeted genetic testing (C9ORF72 / ALS-FTD)

**Protocol ID:** `opendementia-c9orf72-als-ftd-v0.2`  
**Product:** OpenDementia (NeuroAgent)  
**Demo asset:** `public/demo/okn_demo_c9orf72.mp4` (FTD-focused scrub — no alcohol / brainstem / EyeBreathalyzer marketing copy)

## Intended research use

In research use, OpenDementia may **surface OKN / eye-movement impairment-pattern signals associated with some dementia subtypes** (e.g. ALS-FTD spectrum), and help teams **screen patients who may benefit from referral for targeted genetic testing** (starting with **C9ORF72**) for **gene-specific clinical trials**.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Research consent + non-commercial terms |
| 2 | OKN / eye-dynamics session (or demo analysis) |
| 3 | Review research **impairment-pattern / subtype-relevant signals** (illustrative in demo) |
| 4 | Review **priority band for targeted genetic testing** (low / moderate / elevated) |
| 5 | If warranted: **genetic counseling → C9ORF72 lab testing** |
| 6 | Gene-specific trial screening **only after confirmed genotype** + trial criteria |
| 7 | Collaborator form: https://www.theneuroagentai.com/collaborate |

## What this is / is not

| This **is** | This is **not** |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Research signals of **OKN / eye-movement impairment patterns** that may associate with **some dementia subtypes** | A clinical diagnosis of impairment or any dementia subtype |
| Research decision-support for **genetic-testing referral priority** | A genetic test |
| A way to help find candidates for **gene-specific trial pathways** | A diagnosis of ALS/FTD/ALS-FTD |
| Compatible with investigator trial-recruitment workflows | A substitute for counseling, EMG, imaging, or lab genotype |

## Hard limits

- “Impairment” here means **research OKN / eye-movement markers**, not a clinical cognitive-impairment diagnosis.  
- Does **not** detect or confirm C9ORF72 genotype.  
- Does **not** enroll patients in trials.  
- Demo video metrics are **synthetic / illustrative** in this release.
