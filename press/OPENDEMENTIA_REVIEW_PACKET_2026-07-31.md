## 1) What OpenDementia is (current claims)

In **research use only**, OpenDementia:

1. **May surface OKN / eye-movement impairment-pattern signals associated with some dementia subtypes**  
   (research markers — **not** a clinical diagnosis of impairment or any subtype)
2. **Helps prioritize patients for referral to targeted genetic testing** (starting with **C9ORF72** ALS-FTD pathways for gene-specific trial screening)
3. Uses the **HazyEyesIOS MediaPipe OKN** pipeline (camera / Face Mesh / OKN core)

### Hard limits (must not over-claim)

| Do say | Do **not** say |
|--------|----------------|
| Research OKN impairment-**pattern** signals | Diagnoses dementia / MCI / ALS-FTD |
| Some dementia subtypes (research association) | Detects all dementia or alcohol impairment |
| Priority for genetic-**testing referral** | Is a genetic test / reports genotype |
| Gene-specific trial pathway **after** lab genotype | Enrolls patients in trials |
| Research prototype · non-commercial | Medical device · clinical screening tool |
