## NeuroAgent Opens Research Preview of EyeBreathalyzer for Autism and Dementia Research

**Baltimore / Remote — 3 August 2026**

NeuroAgent today opened a research-only preview of **EyeBreathalyzer**, released publicly as **RareNeuroScreen**, for organizations working in **autism**, **dementia**, and **rare neurological disease** research.

The release is designed as a **double launch with one interface**: researchers can switch between an **autism research lane** and a **dementia research lane** without changing the underlying synthetic gaze-dynamics simulator. Only the on-screen language and research framing change by lane. An optional **rare neuro** research lane is also available.

“This is the same EyeBreathalyzer research app interface, opened for the communities that need claim-safe research tooling—not a clinical product pitch,” said the NeuroAgent team. “Autism and dementia research groups can evaluate the preview on the same platform, with lane-specific language and a hard non-diagnostic boundary.”

### What researchers can try

The browser preview lets labs explore **synthetic eye-dynamics** in a controlled, educational setting—including gaze extremes, rebound patterns, and device-agnostic sampling profiles. The autism research lane is scheduled for **public launch on Monday, 3 August 2026**; the dementia lane uses the same application and is ready for co-launch framing.

- Preview: https://neuroagent-eyebreathalyzer.vercel.app  
- Autism lane: https://neuroagent-eyebreathalyzer.vercel.app/?lane=autism  
- Dementia lane: https://neuroagent-eyebreathalyzer.vercel.app/?lane=dementia  

### Clear limits

NeuroAgent is explicit about what the preview is **not**:

- Not a medical device  
- Not for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical screening  
- Not an autism or dementia screening tool  
- Not a license for commercial productization  

Outputs are **synthetic / illustrative** research signals. The public app uses an **access consent** for the research preview. A separate **modular informed-consent master** (v0.9) is maintained for future IRB-localized studies and is **not** auto-deployed as participant enrollment in this preview.

### Who it is for

The release is aimed at **research labs, academic groups, and nonprofit research consortia** evaluating remote neurocognitive research tooling under non-commercial terms.
