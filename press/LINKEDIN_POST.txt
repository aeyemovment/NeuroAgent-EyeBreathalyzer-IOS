NeuroAgent is opening OpenDementia — an open research OKN / MediaPipe eye-tracking app for dementia research organizations.

What’s included:
• Research and education use only (Research Non-Commercial License)
• Not a medical device · not for diagnosis or clinical screening
• Built on the HazyEyesIOS camera OKN pipeline

How to engage:
1) Try the research preview: https://opendementia.vercel.app
2) Fill out the collaborate form so we can follow up: https://www.theneuroagentai.com/collaborate

Research inquiries: info@neuroagentai.org
GitHub: https://github.com/aeyemovment/NeuroAgent-EyeBreathalyzer-IOS
