NeuroAgent is opening a research preview of EyeBreathalyzer — released as RareNeuroScreen — for autism, dementia, and rare neurological disease research organizations.

What’s different about this release:
• Double launch, one interface — autism and dementia research lanes share the same EyeBreathalyzer app
• Lane-specific language only — the synthetic gaze-dynamics simulator does not change by lane
• Built for non-commercial research and education

What this is not:
This preview is not a medical device, not for clinical care, and not an autism or dementia diagnostic or screening tool. Outputs are synthetic / illustrative research signals.

Who it’s for:
Research labs, academic groups, and nonprofit research consortia evaluating remote neurocognitive research tooling.
